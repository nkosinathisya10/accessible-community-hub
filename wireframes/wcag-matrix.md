# WCAG 2.1 AA Matrix

This table shows the WCAG 2.1 AA rules I am using for the clinic booking portal. For each rule I wrote where it applies, how I built it, how I check it, and what I found when I checked it.

"Tests" means my Vitest tests (59 tests, all passing). "NVDA" means my own manual testing with NVDA and Chrome. The full NVDA notes are in test-results/manual-testing-log.md.

| Rule (ID and name) | Part of the page | How I build it | How I check it | What I found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (Level A) | Forms, headings and the service details | Every input has a label that is linked to it. The appointment times and the "How urgent is it?" choices are radio buttons grouped in a fieldset with a legend. The page uses proper parts like header, nav, main, section, article, aside and footer. | Run axe and look for errors. Turn on NVDA and listen to check that it reads the labels and the group names. | Tests: axe found no problems on any page, on the form with errors, or in the popup. A test checks every field has a label and that the times group is named "Time you would like to come". NVDA: it read the main menu as a navigation with a list of 3 items. NVDA (24 November): every form field read its name, "required" and its help text, and the times were read as "Time you would like to come, grouping, required". |
| 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA) | All the text | Normal text must be at least 4.5 to 1 and big text at least 3 to 1. I checked every text colour against every background it sits on: the page, the white cards and popup, the header, the footer, the buttons and the yellow notice. All the numbers are in the comments at the top of main.css. | Work out each pair with the WCAG formula and a contrast checker. Run Lighthouse and look for contrast warnings. | Every text pair passes. The lowest one is the muted help text on the page, at 7.11 to 1. Lighthouse accessibility on 11 October: Home 100, Find a service 100, Book a visit 100. I have not run Lighthouse again since adding the times and the urgency choice, but axe in the tests found no problems on the new parts. |
| 2.1.1 Keyboard (Level A) | The whole site: menu, search, urgency choice, filters, form, times and popup | Use real links, buttons, radio buttons and form fields so Tab, Space, Enter and the arrow keys work. Nothing should only work with a mouse. | Put the mouse away and do a whole booking with only the keyboard. | Tests: the Filters button opens with Enter and Space, the arrow keys move between the times and between the urgency choices, and Escape closes the popup. NVDA and keyboard only: I booked a Sugar check from the services page to the popup using only the keyboard. The arrow keys worked in the urgency choice. In the times, the Right arrow did not change the time in my test (NVDA said "Back"), which I still need to look at again. |
| 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks (Level A) | Top of every page | A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing you get when you press Tab. The page parts also let a screen reader jump straight to the main part. | Press Tab on a new page and check the link shows and works. In NVDA, jump to the main part. | Tests: the skip link is there and points to the main part. NVDA: Tab showed the skip link box and NVDA said "Skip to main content, visited, same page, link". Enter took me to the "main landmark". In my first tries after reloading, Tab started on the menu instead. In a new tab it worked. |
| 2.4.3 Focus Order (Level A) | Menu, form, filters and popup | The page is written in the same order that it looks on the screen. When the page changes, focus moves to the new page heading. When the popup opens, focus moves to its heading. When it is closed with Close or Escape, focus goes back to the "Book my visit" button. When "Find another service" is pressed, that button is gone (the page changes), so focus goes to the "Find a clinic service" heading instead. | Tab through each page and check the order makes sense. Test the popup in the React version, with both Close and "Find another service". | Tests: focus moves to the page heading, into the popup, back to "Book my visit" after Escape, and to the services heading after "Find another service". A test also checks the popup does not try to focus the removed button. NVDA: the popup was read as "Your visit is booked, dialog", Tab went Close, Find another service, Close, and Escape put me back on "Book my visit". I did not test "Find another service" with NVDA yet. |
| 2.4.7 Focus Visible (Level AA) | Every link, button and field | I made my own focus ring. It is 3px thick with a 2px gap. It is blue on the page and on the white cards, and yellow on the dark header and footer. Every ring is at least 3 to 1 against the colour around it. | Tab through each page and check I can always see where I am. Work out the ring contrast for every background. | The contrast audit found one problem: the skip link shows on top of the green header, and the blue ring there was only 1.68 to 1. I changed the skip link to the yellow ring (6.46 to 1). All the other rings pass (the lowest is 4.93 to 1 on the yellow notice). |
| 3.3.1 Error Identification (Level A) | Booking form | If a field is wrong, a message in words shows under it and says what is wrong. The message is linked to the field (aria-describedby) and the field is marked as wrong (aria-invalid). For the times, the whole group is marked as wrong and linked to the message. After a failed submit, focus moves to the first wrong field. | Send the form with empty fields. Send it with only the time missing. Check the message shows and NVDA reads it. | Tests: an empty form shows 6 errors, marks each field invalid and moves focus to Service. With only the time missing, focus goes to the first time and the group is marked invalid with "Please choose a time". NVDA: the empty form read "There are 6 problems with the form", focus went to Service with "invalid entry" and the error, and each field after it said "invalid entry" and its error. NVDA also showed me two problems (errors read out when just tabbing past empty fields, and the time error read again for every service). I fixed both. |
| 4.1.3 Status Messages (Level AA) | Form checking, the number of search results and the urgency message | Messages that change without focus moving go in a live area (aria-live), so a screen reader reads them without moving focus. The results count and the urgency message are in a polite status line. The field errors are polite. The "There are 6 problems" message after pressing Book my visit is assertive. The booking done message is not a live area: it is a dialog, and focus moves to its heading "Your visit is booked", so the screen reader reads it because of the focus move (see 2.4.3). | Type in the search box and check NVDA says how many results there are. Choose "Emergency" and check NVDA reads the message. Send an empty form and check NVDA reads the summary. | Tests: the count, the urgency message and the summary are in live areas with the right politeness. NVDA: typing "baby" read "6 services found" then "2 services found". Choosing Emergency read "Emergency chosen. Do not book online. Call 10177 or 112." straight away. The summary "There are 6 problems with the form" was read out after pressing Book my visit. |

## Notes

- The wireframes have no JavaScript, so some rules (like 3.3.1, 4.1.3 and the popup part of 2.4.3) are only planned in the wireframes. They are built and tested in the React app.
- The test results above are for the React app. The NVDA results come from my own testing on 24 November 2026 and are written down in test-results/manual-testing-log.md.
